# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the VoiceForge community a
welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of experience level,
background or identity. We want a community that is friendly,
respectful and focused on building useful things together.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Give constructive feedback; assume good faith.
- Help newcomers where you can.
- Keep discussion on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Using the project or community spaces to promote voice cloning of
  people without their consent.
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban
participants who break this code. Reports can be made privately to
the maintainer at majumdertanmoy510@gmail.com. All reports will be
reviewed and kept confidential.
